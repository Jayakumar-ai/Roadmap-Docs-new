A Request for Quotation (RFQ) in Roadmap ERP is a formal document used to solicit competitive bids from suppliers for the procurement of goods or services. It specifies the requirements, quantities, and delivery terms needed by the organization. The RFQ facilitates comparison of supplier offers, enabling informed decision-making based on factors such as pricing, quality, and delivery schedules.
