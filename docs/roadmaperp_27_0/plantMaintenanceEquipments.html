Equipment is a term that refers to any physical asset or machinery that is used in the production or operation of a plant. Equipment can include various types of machines, devices, components, or systems that perform different functions or tasks. Equipment can have different characteristics, such as size, shape, capacity, power, speed, efficiency, accuracy, etc.

The Equipment & Description are defined in this screen. The Equipment cannot be modified or deleted when the schedule was created.

Navigation path:

Option 1: Type the business function ID in search box and click on the OK button.

Option 2: Plant Maintenance \ Transactions \ Equipments
