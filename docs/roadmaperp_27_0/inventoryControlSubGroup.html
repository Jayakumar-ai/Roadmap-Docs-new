A Sub Group is a way of grouping items that have the same sub-category, such as type, size, color, material, or usage. A Sub Group helps to refine and differentiate the items more precisely and accurately.

The Item Sub Group and Sub Group Description are defined in this screen. The Sub Group cannot be modified or deleted when there exist child records.

Navigation path:

Supply Chain Management \ Inventory \ Setup \ Setup - Inventory

  • Click button to create a Sub Group
  • Enter Sub Group.
  • Enter the Item Codification
  • Enter Print Seq. No.
  • Enter/Select the group from LOV; Grp.Print Seq.No. will be displayed automatically.
  • Click to Save the Sub group.
