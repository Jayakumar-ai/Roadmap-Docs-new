Visitor Gate Pass

A visitor gate pass is a document or card issued to visitors when they enter a facility or premises. It serves as a temporary identification and authorization for the visitor to access specific areas within the facility. The visitor gate pass ensures that only authorized individuals can enter and helps maintain security and accurate records of all visitors.
