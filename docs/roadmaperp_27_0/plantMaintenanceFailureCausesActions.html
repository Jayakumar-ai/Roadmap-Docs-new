This screen is used to classify the failures that occur in the plant maintenance process and to link them with their respective categories. A category is a group of failures that share a common cause, effect, or solution. For example, a category could be electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, etc. A failure is a specific problem or malfunction that affects the equipment or components. For example, a failure could be a short circuit, a broken valve, a leak, etc.

The category in this screen and the failures defined are associated with its corresponding category. The Category cannot be modified or deleted when the child record exists.
