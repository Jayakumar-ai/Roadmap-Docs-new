A characteristic is a way of describing or defining a certain property or attribute of a plant equipment or machinery. A characteristic can have different types, values, formats, and units depending on the nature and function of the equipment or machinery.

The Characteristic are defined in this screen.

Navigation path:

Plant Maintenance \ Setup \ Setup-Plant Maintenance \ Characteristics

  • Click to Add.
  • Enter the Characteristic Description
  • Select the Type (Variable or Attribute)
  • Click on button to save
