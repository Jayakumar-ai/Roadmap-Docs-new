Supplier Performance in Roadmap ERP tracks and evaluates the reliability and efficiency of the suppliers. This feature helps to monitor key metrics such as delivery times, quality of goods, and compliance with contractual terms.

Navigation path:

Supply Chain Management \ Purchasing \ Setup \ Setup-Purchase \ Suplr. Performance

  • Click to create suplr. performance.
  • Select the unit from the LOV.
  • Check flag of the Performance Rating Factors and Enter the Percentage of Factors.
  • Click on to save.
  • Click to create.
  • Select the Unit and Doc prefix.
  • Enter/Select the Effective From and Effective To date.
  • Give supplier Range values and choose the Grade field.
  • Click on to save.
  • Click Activate button The Document will be activated.
  • If Required to copy the item and supplier for another Unit.
  • Give the Attribute values and score level.
  • Click on to save.
