The Party Offsets is a feature that allows the user to match the documents that have either debit or credit balances with each other, in order to clear the balances and reconcile the accounts. The offset will show the total amount of debit and credit documents, the net balance, and the status of the offset.

All the adjusted document details are displayed in this screen.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transactions \ Payment & Receipts \ Pending Dr. Doc.\ Pending Receivables

This screen shows the Adjustment details.

  • In this screen, the adjusted details are viewed.
  • Click on the Details button to view the document number and adjusted amount.
  • The adjusted amount can be reversed. Once the amount is reversed, the same will be reflected in Creditors payables.
  • The adjusted line can be deleted and if the line is deleted, the line is moved to History tab.
