The Open/Blanket Purchase Order screen is used to establish the contracted price of items with a specific supplier. This feature allows for setting agreed-upon pricing terms and conditions for ongoing or future purchases, ensuring consistency and clarity in procurement transactions.

Navigation path:

Supply Chain Management \ Purchasing \ Transactions \ Open PO/RC

  • Open PO/RC Amendment option is to amend the blanket PO.
