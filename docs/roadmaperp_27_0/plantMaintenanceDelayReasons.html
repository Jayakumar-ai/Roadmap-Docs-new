Delay reason is a term that describes the cause or factor that prevents or postpones a maintenance task from being completed on time. Ex: Unforeseen equipment failures, Parts shortages, Labor shortages, scheduling conflicts, permits and approvals etc.

The Delay Reasons are defined in this screen.
