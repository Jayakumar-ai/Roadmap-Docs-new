Adjustments are made for the payments for which we have given the advance amounts, after adjusting the balance payment are made to the supplier.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transactions \ Payment & Receipts \ Pending Cr. Doc.\ Pending Payables

  • Search and find the debit and credit documents to be adjusted.
  • Enter the amount to be adjusted and select both the Debit and credit Document.
  • Click Adjust button.
  • Enter/Select the date to be adjusted and click Ok.
  • The document will be adjusted and the adjusted documents will be viewed in the party Offsets screen.
