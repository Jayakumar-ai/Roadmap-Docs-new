The Instrument Groups can be defined in this screen. This screen is used to add, modify, and delete Instrument Groups.

Navigation Path:

Quality Control \ Setup \ Setup – Quality

Instruments Group:

  • Click to create.
  • Enter the Group Description.
  • Click to save.

Modify Instruments Group:

  • Click on the Instruments Group.
  • Change the Description.
  • Click to save.

Disable Instruments Group:

  • Only unused Instruments Groups can be disabled.
  • Disable the Active flag.
