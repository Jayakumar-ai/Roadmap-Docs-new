Fixed assets are long-term tangible/in tangible assets that a company owns and uses in its operations to generate income. These assets are not expected to be consumed or converted into cash within a year. Examples of fixed assets include buildings, machinery, vehicles, and equipment.

This screen is used to configure Fixed assets.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Setup \ GST/FA/Costing \ Setup-Fixed Assets

  • Select the Change Depr To as (i) Asset count or (ii) Accumulated Depreciation.
  • Click to save.

Master Group

  • Click to add.
  • Enter the Master Group.
  • Select Type.
  • Enable Active checkbox.

Groups

  • Enter the Group.

Sub Group

  • If deprecation is allowed for asset, enable Depreciation Allowed Checkbox.
