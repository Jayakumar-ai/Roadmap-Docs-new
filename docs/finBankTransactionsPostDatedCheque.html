Post Dated cheque means a cheque which had been written by the drawer for a future date.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transactions \ Payment & Receipts \ Post Dated Cheque

  • While creating the Payment document in bank, enter the Cheque date fields as any future date. While completing the entry, check the Post dated cheque checkbox.
  • After approving, the status will be displayed as Posted and the document will be displayed in Post Dated Cheque screen.
  • On the cheque date entered, select the document and Post the same.
  • Then the document status will be changed as Issued. Journals will be created on the Post dated cheque date.

The above concept is applicable for Receipt voucher document as well. In receipts, the past dates and future date can be given. Val. Date will be populated as Cheque date in both Payments and receipts.
