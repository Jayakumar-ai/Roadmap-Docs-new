PNL/BS Heads

Click Schedule III button to configure PNL/BS Heads.

  • Click to add
  • Enter the Head ID.
  • Enter the Name.
  • Choose a type as Profit and Loss or Balance Sheet.
  • Click to save

PNL/BS Heads Config.

This screen is used to create Profit and Loss/Balance Sheet heads configuration. A configuration is a set of rules or parameters that determines how the heads are displayed, calculated, and grouped in the financial statements.

Click Schedule III button to configure PNL/BS Heads config.

  • Select the type as PNL/BS Heads config.
  • Click on Search button.
  • Click on the necessary Head.
  • Click to add.
  • Enter the Type.
  • Enter the Head and Name.
  • Click to save
  • Click on Active checkbox.
