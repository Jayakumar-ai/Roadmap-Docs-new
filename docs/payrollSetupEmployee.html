Employee profile–related master data can be configured using this screen.

Payroll \ Setup \ Setup-Employee

Educations:

Education details based on organizational requirements can be defined in this screen.

  • Click to create.
  • Enter education details.
  • Select education level.
  • Click to save; the Active checkbox will be enabled automatically.

Branches of Study:

  • Click to create.
  • Enter branch of study.
  • Click to save; the Active checkbox will be enabled automatically.
  • To delete a branch, click the Delete button.

Note: If the record is associated with child records, the parent record cannot be deleted.

  • Click to create.

  • Click to save; the Active checkbox will be enabled automatically.

Designations:

Employee designations and hierarchy levels can be defined in this screen.

  • Click to create.
  • Enter designation level to create basic hierarchy.
  • Click to save; the Active checkbox will be enabled automatically.

Designation Group:

To create group based on the designation user can use this screen.

  • Click to create.
  • Enter designation group.
  • Enter design level.
  • Click to save; the Active checkbox will be enabled automatically.

Designation:

To map the designation and designation group user can us this screen and also over time can be enabled in this screen.

  • Click to create.
  • Enter Design ID.
  • Designation group and level will be fetched automatically.
  • Enable Allow Overtime checkbox as per company policy.
  • Click to save; the Active checkbox will be enabled automatically.

Salary and Allowance:

Payroll elements based on CTC range and grade can be defined in this screen.

  • Click to create.
  • Select Effective From and Effective To dates.
  • Select category.
  • Click to save the header.
  • Click to create grade lines.
  • Enter grade.
  • Click to create Basic / Allowance.
  • Enter element.
  • Enter minimum and maximum values.
  • Click to save.

Workday Calendar:

To declare, week-off and holidays for the financials year can be defined using this screen.

  • Click to create.
  • Select calendar and year; start and end dates will be fetched automatically.
  • Select zone.
  • Click to save the header.
  • Select applicable week-offs.
  • Click to save.
  • Click Generate Calendar to create the calendar.
  • Click Activate to activate the calendar.
