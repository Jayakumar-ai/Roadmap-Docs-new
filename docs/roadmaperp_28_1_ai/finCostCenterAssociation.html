This screen is used to define the cost center to capture the revenue and expenses against an entity or a unit. A cost center is a segment of the business that is responsible for generating costs, but not for generating revenue.

This screen is used to create Partner cost center association.

  • Click CPC Association Ratio button in the find screen to associate the CPC.

Cost Center Association (Entity)

  • Click to add.
  • Select/Enter the unit with levels.
  • If your balance sheet level is Entity, to associate the cost center at Entity level.
  • Click to save.

Cost Center Association (Unit)

  • Click to add.
  • Select/Enter the unit with levels.
  • If your balance sheet level is unit, to associate the cost center at unit level.
  • Click to save.

Cost Center Association (Purchase)

  • Click to add.
  • Select/ enter the Trans unit .
  • Select / Enter the Item
  • Select/Enter the Class, Sub class
  • Select/Enter the CPC Desc.
  • Enable the active button
  • If you need separate cost center to be maintained for Purchase it can be defined here.
  • Click to save.
