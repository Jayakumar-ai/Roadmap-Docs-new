Customers are the people or entities that owe money to a company for the goods or services that they have purchased or used on credit. Customers are also known as debtors, receivables, or trade receivables.

They are recorded as an asset on the company's balance sheet, because they represent a legal claim to future cash payments. Customers are usually classified by their payment terms, such as net 30, net 60, or net 90 days.

Customers can also be grouped by their industry, location, credit rating, or other criteria. The management of customers involves issuing invoices, collecting payments, offering discounts, applying interest charges, writing off bad debts, and reporting on the status and performance of the receivables.

This screen is used to define the Customers Data and it is used to add, modify and delete Customers.
