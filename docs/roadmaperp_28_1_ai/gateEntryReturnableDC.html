A Returnable DC documents the movement of goods which are expected to be returned to the sender after their use. For ex: manufacturer sending out special tooling or molds to a supplier for job work, expecting them to be returned after the job is completed.

Navigation path:

Supply Chain Management \ Gate Entry \ Transaction \ Returnable DC
