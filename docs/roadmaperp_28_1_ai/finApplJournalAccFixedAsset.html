This screen is used to define Application journal Accounts for Fixed Assets (FA).

Navigation path:

Financial Management \ Setup \ CPC/GL account \ Appl. Accts (fixed asset).

  • Click to add.
  • Enter/Select a Sub Group from LOV.
  • Enter SLM (CO). Details.
  • Enter WDV(CO) Details.
  • Click to save the header.
  • In the Line Item, Click to add.
  • Enter/Select an account Type from LOV.
  • Enter/Select Account.
  • Click to save.
