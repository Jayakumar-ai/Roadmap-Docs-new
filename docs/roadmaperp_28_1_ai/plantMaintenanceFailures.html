Equipment failure is a term that describes any situation where a piece of equipment does not work as intended or expected. It can mean that the equipment stops working completely, works partially, or works poorly.

The Equipment's failure & Description are defined in this screen. The failure cannot be modified or deleted when there exist child records, but the descriptions can be modified.
