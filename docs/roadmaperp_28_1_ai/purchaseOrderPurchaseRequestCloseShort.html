The Purchase Request close short or amendment form allows you to close short or amendment the Approved and Partial Purchase Request lines. It is made possible only when the particular request lines were open and not yet have been converted into Purchase Orders.Once the request is close shorted or cancelled, the requested qty in the requested bucket will be decreased in stocks. In Bulk Closeshort the corresponding purchase request document wlll be close shorted.

Navigation path:

Supply Chain \ Purchasing \ Transaction \ PR /SSR CS/Amendment
