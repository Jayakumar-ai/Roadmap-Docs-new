A Subclass is a category or type of inventory items that have more specific characteristics than the Class they belong to. A Subclass helps to refine and differentiate the inventory items more precisely and accurately. A Subclass can also have child records, which are the inventory items that are assigned to the Subclass.

The Subclasses are defined in this screen and the classes defined are associated with its corresponding subclasses.
