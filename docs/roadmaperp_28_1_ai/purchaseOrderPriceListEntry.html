Price List Entry in Roadmap ERP allows users to define and manage pricing information for products or services by the Suppliers. It enables the entry of item-specific prices, discounts, and effective dates to ensure accurate and up-to-date pricing across transactions.

Navigation path:

Supply Chain \ Purchasing \ Transaction \ Price List Entry
