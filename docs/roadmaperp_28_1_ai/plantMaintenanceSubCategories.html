Sub Categories

The Sub Category and the Category defined are associated with its corresponding sub Category. The sub Category cannot be modified or deleted when the child record exists, but the descriptions can be modified.

Navigation path:

Plant Maintenance \ Setup \ Setup-Plant Maintenance \ Category \ Sub Categories

  • Click to add
  • Enter the Sub category.
  • Enter/Select the Category Description gets displayed automatically
  • Enter the Sub category Prefix.
  • Enter the Next Number.
  • Click to save
  • Function of the Template button is to download the template in Excel format.
  • Click Upload button helps to upload the files.
  • Click Download button shows the details in Excel format.
  • View Exception shows the Exception details.
