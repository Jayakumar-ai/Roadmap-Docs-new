A linearity study in quality management is part of Measurement System Analysis. It evaluates whether the measurement system's bias (the difference between the measured value and the true value) remains consistent across the entire range of measurements. Essentially, it checks if the measurement system performs uniformly, regardless of the size or scale of the measurement. This screen is used to view documents that are listed for Linearity Study.
