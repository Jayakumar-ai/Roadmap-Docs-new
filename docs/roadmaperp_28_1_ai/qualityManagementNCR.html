During the Inspection transaction, there exists rejection of quantities. The root cause of the defect occurred, Impacts of the defect and the necessary corrective action to be done for the defects are created as document for the supplier/customer involved in the transaction. Such a document is called Non-conformance and corrective action record. The document can be created automatically or manually based on the NCR control.
