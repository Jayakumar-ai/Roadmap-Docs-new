Gate entry inward/outward refers to the processes of recording and managing the movement of goods, materials, and items entering (inward) or leaving (outward) a facility. These processes are crucial for maintaining accurate inventory records, ensuring security, and streamlining logistics

This screen is used to record the Inward/Outward of the organization.
