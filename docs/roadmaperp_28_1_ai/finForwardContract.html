A forward contract is a customized agreement between two parties to buy or sell an asset at a specified price on a future date. Unlike standardized futures contracts traded on exchanges, forward contracts are private and can be tailored to meet the specific needs of the parties involved. They are commonly used for hedging purposes, allowing companies to lock in prices for commodities or currencies, thereby reducing the risk of price fluctuations.
