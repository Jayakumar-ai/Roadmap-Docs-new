FIN Period setup:

This screen is used to define financial periods. Financial periods are intervals of time that are used for accounting and reporting purposes. They can be monthly, quarterly, yearly, or any other duration that suits the business needs. Financial periods help the business to measure and compare its performance, plan its budget, and comply with the tax and legal requirements.

Navigation path:

Financials\setup \Fin. Year/ Periods \Fin. Periods

Open/Close Fin.Period – Click this button to start,update or to close the Financial Period.

Fin.Period Setup – Click this button to show the Financial Periods Description details.

Non-Statutory Fin.Period – Click this button to configure the non-statutory financial period.
