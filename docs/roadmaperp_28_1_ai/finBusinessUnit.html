A business unit refers to a distinct division within a company that operates independently from the core business or brand. It develops and implements its own processes while still adhering to the overall company policies.

This screen is used to create a Business Unit.

Navigation path:

Setup \ Business Entity \ Business Unit

  • User can view all the created business Units in this screen.
  • Click to create a new Business Unit.
  • Enter the Business Unit ID/Name Description.
  • Select the BE from LOV.
  • Select Unit Group from LOV.
  • Enter the Address.
  • Select city from LOV.
  • Select Type - Manufacturing.
  • Enter the GST Details.
  • After given the All details click to save.
  • Click Activate button to activate the Business unit.

Location

  • User can view all the created location in this screen.
  • Click to create a new a new Location.
  • Enter Loc ID.
  • Enter location name.
  • Select the Unit & City from LOV.
  • Click to save.
  • Check Dflt.Loc. checkbox for Unit Location and Check the Active checkbox.

Unit Group

  • Click to create a new Unit group.
  • Enter Unit Group name.
  • Click to save and the active checkbox will be enabled automatically.
  • Enter the Group name and click on the Active checkbox to activate the Unit Group.
  • User can download the unit group details by clicking the download button , with options such as CSV, HTML, PDF and Excel.
