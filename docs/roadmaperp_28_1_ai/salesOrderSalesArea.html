Sales area, sales Territories, sales Sub Territories, sales Person, and sales Price Class, Catalog are defined in this screen.

Navigation path

Supply Chain\Sales\Setup\Setup-Sales\Sales Area

Sales Region

Figure 1.5

  • Click to create a new Sales Area
  • Enter the Sales Area Name
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Area Manager Name.
  • Function of the Template button is to download the template and to enter the details of the Resource in Excel Format for bulk upload.
  • Upload button helps to upload the files.
  • Download button shows the details in Excel format.
  • Save the Sales Area & Finally Check the Active Flag.

Modify Sales Area

  • Uncheck Active Flag
  • Make the necessary modification
  • Save the Sales Area
  • Activate the Flag again

Note: Modification can be made to the Region that are new and if there are any child records the modification cannot be done.

Sales Zone

Navigation path

Supply Chain\Sales\Setup\Setup-Sales\Sales Zone

Figure 1.6

  • Click to create a new Sales Zone.
  • Enter the Sales Zone
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Area.
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Manager Name.
  • Click on Template option to download the template for territories
  • Click on to upload the territories
  • Click on to export the territories to excel format
  • Click the Territory & Finally Check the Active flag.
