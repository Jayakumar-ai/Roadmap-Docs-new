Sales area, sales Territories, sales Sub Territories, sales Person, and sales Price Class, Catalog are defined in this screen.

Sales Areas

Navigation Path

Supply Chain \ Sales \ Setup \ Setup-Sales \ Sales Area

  • Click to create a new Sales Area.
  • Enter the Sales Area Name.
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Area Manager Name.
  • Function of the Template button is to download the template and to enter the details of the Resource in Excel Format for bulk upload.
  • Upload button helps to upload the files.
  • Download button shows the details in Excel format.
  • Click to save the Sales Area & Finally Check the Active Flag.

Modify Sales Area

  • Uncheck Active Flag.
  • Make the necessary modification.
  • Save the Sales Area.
  • Activate the Flag again.

Note:Modification can be made to the Area that are new and if there are any child records the modification cannot be done.

Territories

Navigation Path

Supply Chain \ Sales \ Setup \ Setup-Sales \ Territory

  • Click to create a new Territory.
  • Enter the Territory.
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Area.
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Manager Name.
  • Click on Template option to download the template for territories.
  • Click on upload icon to upload the territories.
  • Click on export icon to export the territories to excel format.
  • Click to save the Territory & Finally Check the Active flag.

Sub Territories

Navigation Path

Supply Chain \ Sales \ Setup \ Setup-Sales \ Sub Territory

  • Click to create a new Sub Territory.
  • Enter the Sub Territory.
  • Click the LOV button and select Territory.
  • Click the LOV button and select Manager.
  • Click the LOV button and select Sales Person.
  • Enable the Active Flag.
  • Click on Template to download the template for sub territories.
  • Click on upload icon to upload the sub territories.
  • Click on export icon to export the sub territories to excel format.
  • Click to save the Sub Territory.

Sales Persons

Navigation Path

Supply Chain \ Sales \ Setup \ Setup-Sales \ Sales Person

  • Click to create a new Sales Person.
  • Enter the Sales Person.
  • Choose the Sales Person Type.
  • Click the LOV button and select Employee ID.
  • Click the LOV button and select Sub Territory.
  • Select the Category type.
  • If required the address can be updated against the sales person by clicking Address.
  • Finally enable the Active flag & Default.
  • Click on template to download the template for Sales Person.
  • Click on upload icon to upload the Sales Person.
  • Click on export icon to export the Sales Person to excel format.
  • Click to save the Sales Person.

Price Classes

Navigation Path

Supply Chain \ Sales \ Setup \ Setup-Sales \ Price Class

  • Click to create a new Price class.
  • Enter the Price Classes.
  • Finally enable the Active flag & Default.
  • Click to save the Price class.

Catalog

Navigation Path

Supply Chain \ Sales \ Setup \ Setup-Sales \ Catalog

  • Click to create a new Catalog.
  • Enter the Catalog Desc.
  • Finally Check the Active Flag.
