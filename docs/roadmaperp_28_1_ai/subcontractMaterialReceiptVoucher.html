A Material Receipt Voucher is a document used to record the receipt of materials or goods into inventory. It details information such as the supplier, quantity received, condition of goods, and any relevant remarks. This voucher serves as a formal acknowledgment of the receipt of goods and is essential for maintaining accurate inventory records and facilitating seamless accounting processes within the ERP system.
