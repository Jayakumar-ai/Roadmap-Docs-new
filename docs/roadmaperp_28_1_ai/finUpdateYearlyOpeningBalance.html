A yearly opening balance is the amount of money that a company has in their accounts at the beginning of a financial year. This screen is used to view an update yearly opening balance.

All the adjusted document details are displayed in this screen.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transaction \ Fin. Year Closing \ Upd.Yrly.Op.

Upd. Yrly. Op. Bal. (STD)

  • Enter the current year In LOV.
  • Enter the unit in LOV And enter the document date and narration.
  • After that saving header level document and need to load document.
  • Both credit and debit values will be updated.
  • Current year closing for asset, liability and Equity only loaded, For expenses and Revenue value updated in Profit and loss account.
  • Click the post button to updated the opening balance for the next financial year.

Closing stock is the value of the inventory that a company or a business has at the end of a financial year or a reporting period. Closing stock is also known as ending inventory or final inventory. This screen is used to view a closing stock.

All the adjusted document details are displayed in this screen.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transaction \ Fin. Year closing \ Closing stock

  • Select the unit.
  • Select the location.
  • Enter the closing date.
  • Select closing stock account.
  • Enter the closing stock value.
