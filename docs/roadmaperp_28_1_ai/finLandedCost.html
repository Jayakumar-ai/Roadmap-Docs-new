Landed cost is the total cost of a product when it arrives at its destination after being shipped from a supplier. It includes the original price of the product, plus any shipping, insurance, customs, and other fees that are incurred along the way, Landed cost can be calculated using the following formula:

Landed cost = Product cost + Shipping cost + Customs cost + Risk cost + Overhead cost

This screen used to list out the Landed Cost Bills.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transactions \ Party Documents \ Purchase Bills

  • Select the create button.
