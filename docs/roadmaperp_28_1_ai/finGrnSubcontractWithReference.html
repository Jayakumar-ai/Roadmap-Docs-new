A subcontract GRN records the receipt of the finished products from the vendor, and also updates the consumption of the materials that were sent to the vendor.

The GRN which are received against subcontract will be loaded in this screen; the invoice can be created from this Screen.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transactions \ Party Document \ Purchase Bills

Registering Invoice

  • Click on Pending SCO button.
  • Search the Record by using the GRN No. and Records based on date.
  • Select checkbox and click Create.
  • Select Inv.Pfx from Lov.
  • Enter/Select Inv. Date.
  • Click ok.
  • Invoices can be registered in this screen.
  • Click post button and the document can be forwarded for approval.

Process

The subcontract process can be viewed in this tab.

Invoice Lines

Based on the process the item details will be summarized in this tab based on the item and Unit cost.

  • Click on process button to update the TDS under section.
