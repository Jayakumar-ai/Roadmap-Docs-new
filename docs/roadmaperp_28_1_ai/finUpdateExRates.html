Exchange rates are the values of one currency in terms of another currency. Exchange rates are determined by the supply and demand of currencies in the foreign exchange market, as well as by other factors such as interest rates, inflation, trade, and political stability. Exchange rates affect the prices of goods and services that are traded across borders, as well as the value of foreign investments and assets.

This Screen is used to Update the Exchange rate.

Navigation path:

Financials \ Transactions \ Ex.Rates \ Update Ex. Rates

  • Click to add a new Exchange rate
  • Enter Currency ID, Date, Import (Purchase) Exchange rate and Export (Sales) Exchange rate.
  • Click to Save.
