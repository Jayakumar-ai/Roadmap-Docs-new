Causes in equipment failures are the factors or events that lead to the malfunction or breakdown of a piece of equipment, resulting in partial or complete loss of its intended value.

The Causes are defined in this screen. The cause cannot be modified or deleted when there exist child records, but the descriptions can be modified.
