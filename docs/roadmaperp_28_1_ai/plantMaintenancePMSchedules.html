A plant maintenance schedule is a plan that outlines the tasks and frequency of maintenance activities for a plant or facility. It is intended to prevent breakdowns, improve performance, and extend the life of equipment and systems.

The Equipment is activated and the schedule is created based on the variable days or meter reading.
